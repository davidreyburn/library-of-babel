GPU conformance

Runs the shader's own copy of the lattice — the GLSL in core/babel-glsl.mjs, the exact text the prototype embeds — over the cells in core/vectors.json, and compares what the GPU computes against what core/babel-core.mjs computes on the CPU. The GLSL is a port, not a copy: it has no Math.imul and no 53-bit doubles, so the same arithmetic is written differently and can only be trusted by being run. Results come back through a RGBA32UI framebuffer, so these are the integers themselves, not pixels inspected by eye.
