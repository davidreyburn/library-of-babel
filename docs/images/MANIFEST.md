# Screenshots

Captured through Chrome while building, 1–2 August 2026. The numbered images are
the milestones referenced from `docs/CASE-STUDY.md` and `docs/BUG-LOG.md`;
`archive/` holds every shot taken, chronologically by the timestamp in the
filename, unlabelled.

| | what it shows |
|---|---|
| `01-first-render.jpg` | The first walkable render, 1 Aug 18:10. Books are banded stacks rather than spines, there are no distinct shelf carcasses, the palette is a single pale yellow-green with no wood, the controls are still *ascend / descend*, and the HUD is showing mojibake where the quotation marks should be. Everything after this is iteration. |
| `02-gap-grammar.jpg` | 2 Aug 00:49. The gap grammar has arrived — *passage · passage · stair down · stair down · stair up* — the shelf boards have warmed to wood, and the encoding is fixed. Spines still band, and a stair is rendering as fragmented geometry at the right. |
| `03-stairwell-corridor.jpg` | 2 Aug 01:58. Inside a stairwell: one flight straight across the cell, a 1.24 m corridor cut in rock rather than an open well. |
| `04-reading-room-lamp.jpg` | 2 Aug 12:10. A reading room, two floors down, found by a wander that walked 34 steps looking for a chair. The lamp is a real light source. |
| `05-reading-room-desk.jpg` | 2 Aug 12:28. The same class of room after the reading-room performance fix: 95 fps at 1:1, where it had been 15.7 ms a frame. |
| `06-first-readable-volume.jpg` | 2 Aug 12:41. The first legible page — the crimson volume, wall 1, shelf 2, slot 17 of cell 15,94 — in the first version of the pane, before the paper treatment. Type at 9.3 px. |
| `07-ink-on-paper.jpg` | 2 Aug 13:14. The pane rebuilt as ink on rag paper: 22 px type, line gutter, pinned column ruler. The grid is drawn because the address *is* an offset. |
| `08-reticule-and-panel.jpg` | 2 Aug 13:29. The reticule on slot 19, its spine named in the address panel, with the crimson volume two slots to the left and an empty slot further along. |
| `09-highlight-zoom.png` | 2 Aug 13:30. Close on the same shelf: the targeted spine lifted toward lit paper, the crimson volume beside it, and the notch where the Purifiers left a gap. |
| `10-on-target-grazing.jpg` | 2 Aug 13:38. The parallax fix, at a 60° grazing angle — the case the old single-plane ray got wrong by six books. The arrow is the OS pointer composited by the capture tool; the page hides its own cursor. |
