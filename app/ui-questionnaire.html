A UI kit for the Library

Nineteen decisions the interface is currently making by accident.

Every “as it stands” below is read off the shipped build, not remembered. Answer what you have an opinion about and skip the rest — a blank is a real answer and means “you choose”. Copy answers at the bottom dumps everything as text to paste back.

IWhat the interface is for

Everything else follows from this, so it comes first.

1. Who is the interface addressing?

As it stands it addresses two readers at once. The address panel talks to a librarian — Through the gaps, Pointing at, You are here — while the perf panel talks to an engineer: 20.0 ms · worst 20.2, 1:2, auto, noise. Both are on screen permanently.

2. Is the interface part of the fiction, or outside it?

Currently mixed. A reading room. No shelves at all: an end table. is inside the fiction. 2 shelved walls: this gallery departs from ‘all sides except two.’ steps outside it to compare the build against Borges. mouse free · click to re-capture is neither.

4. Should a panel be dismissible, and does that state persist?

No panel can be closed. There is no persistence of any kind — no localStorage, so the grain and scale selects reset on every reload.

5. The address panel is nine rows deep and always open. Too much?

Floor · Cell · rule · Shelved · Volumes here · Through the gaps · rule · a note · Pointing at · You are here · This exact view. The note (Every floor is a different network…) never changes.

7. R is described only as “if stuck”. What is it for?

The help says R if stuck and nothing else. A reader who has never been stuck has no idea what it does or whether it will lose their place.

8. The two selects are unlabelled.

auto | 1:1 | 1:2 | 1:3 and ordered | noise | fine | smooth, side by side, with no captions. You cannot tell which is resolution and which is dither without changing one. Also a real bug: the auto-scaler now goes to 1:4, but the manual list still stops at 1:3 — I raised one and not the other.

IVLayout and density

Three corners are occupied; the fourth is empty.

9. Panels sit at fixed corners. Should that be the rule?

Address top-left, performance top-right, help bottom-left, bottom-right empty. Nothing adapts to viewport size; on a narrow window the panels overlap the view.

10. How dense should a panel be?

Type is ~11px mono at 1.65 line-height, keys in --faint and values in --pale, rules between groups. It reads like a catalogue card.

VAppearance

The palette is already decided; what is undecided is how the UI uses it.

11. Should panels sit on the scene or in it?

Panels are rgba(12,14,10,.86) over the canvas with a 1px rule — floating cards, clearly not part of the room.

12. Two typefaces are in play. Which does what?

Serif (Iowan Old Style) is used only on the intro veil's title. Everything else is mono. Book pages are mono because the corpus is fixed-width by construction.

13. What is crimson allowed to mean?

--crimson is used for the one crimson volume in the whole Library (V-09), for focus outlines, and for the reading-room label. Three unrelated meanings.

VILanguage

The copy has a strong voice already. The question is which parts are the standard.

14. Sentence case, lower case, or small caps for labels?

Currently mixed: Through the gaps (sentence), PASSAGE → STAIRS (caps), nothing shelved (lower), CLICK TO ENTER (caps).

15. How long may a tooltip be?

The This exact view tooltip is 458 characters and explains the design rationale for keeping the camera out of the address. The mouse tooltip explains iframe sandboxing. These are essays in title attributes — invisible on touch, and unreadable to a screen reader in that form.

16. What voice is the UI written in?

Present tense, second person, no exclamation, occasional quotation of the source text: 2 shelved walls: this gallery departs from ‘all sides except two.’ and X to stop · WASD takes over. Errors are plain: that stair is a dead end.

17. How should a refusal read?

Refusals currently state the reason and stop: that stair is a dead end, this gallery has no shelved wall, nothing shelved. None of them suggests what to do next.
