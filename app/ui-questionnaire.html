as it stands — a hover essay

x, y, z, yaw, pitch — metres and radians, at full precision so the camera is restored exactly. It rides in the page’s own query string rather than in the babel:// address, because every part of an address is checkable (a wall that is a doorway is refused) and where you happen to be looking is not.
